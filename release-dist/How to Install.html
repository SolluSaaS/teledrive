⚡ TeleDrive for macOS

Installation Guide

Quick setup instructions for Apple Silicon (M1/M2/M3/M4) & Intel Macs

Recommended • 1-Click Setup

⚡ Option 1: Automatic 1-Click Installer

Bypasses macOS Gatekeeper download verification automatically without requiring any manual Terminal commands.

1
Double-click Install TeleDrive.command in this disk image.
2
Terminal will open and automatically copy TeleDrive into /Applications, clear the download quarantine flag, and launch the app!
3
Once finished, close Terminal and enjoy TeleDrive!

📂 Option 2: Traditional Drag-and-Drop

Standard macOS drag-and-drop installation into your Applications folder.

1
Drag TeleDrive into the Applications shortcut folder.
2
Open TeleDrive from your Applications folder or Spotlight.
⚠️ If macOS displays "cannot be verified" or "Apple cannot check for malicious software":

Because TeleDrive is free, open-source software, macOS Gatekeeper may show a security notice on first launch:

1. Open System Settings  → Privacy & Security.
2. Scroll down to the Security section.
3. Click "Open Anyway" next to TeleDrive.

(Or in Terminal, simply run: xattr -cr /Applications/TeleDrive.app)

TeleDrive is 100% free and open-source. Your Telegram session and storage keys remain strictly on your local device.
